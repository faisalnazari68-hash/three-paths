Privacy Policy

Three Paths · Last updated: November 27, 2026

Three Paths is an educational app for reading and learning about Christianity, Islam and Hinduism. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not require an account, and it does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, bookmarks, text-size and display settings, quiz best score and last-read position are saved only in your device's local storage so the app can remember them. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Internet use

All scripture texts, fonts and content are bundled with the app, so it works offline. Some pages include links to outside websites (for example, public-domain translations of Hindu texts on sacred-texts.com). If you tap one, that website's own privacy practices apply.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
